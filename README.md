## How I Approach Engineering

I believe good engineering is not about using the most technologies. It is about making the right technical decisions.

My approach emphasizes:

- **Architecture first:** Understand requirements, constraints, and trade-offs before implementation.
- **Pragmatic technology selection:** Choose tools based on actual needs rather than trends.
- **Security by design:** Treat authentication, authorization, and data protection as architectural concerns.
- **Automation:** Reduce repetitive work through reproducible processes and tooling.
- **Maintainability:** Build systems that are understandable, testable, and adaptable.
- **Continuous learning:** Explore emerging technologies while maintaining strong engineering fundamentals.
